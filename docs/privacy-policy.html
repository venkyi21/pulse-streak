Privacy Policy — Pulse Streak

Last updated: 1 August 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.pulsestreak.game

The short version. Pulse Streak collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. It does not send any information anywhere, because it does not communicate over the internet at all. Your game progress is saved on your own device and never leaves it.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Accounts or login credentials — there is no sign-in of any kind
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP-based identifiers
  • Photos, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or SMS
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural telemetry

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally, using your device's standard app storage, under a single key named pulse_streak_save_v1. It contains only gameplay values:

  • Your best score and collected in-game currency ("shards")
  • Which in-game upgrades and cosmetic colours you have unlocked
  • Your daily play streak and remaining energy
  • A local high-score list, including any 3-letter initials you choose to enter

This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted, backed up to us, or accessible to us or to anyone else. Any initials you type are purely local and are never published or shared. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, permanently erases all of it.

Network access

Pulse Streak works entirely offline. It makes no network requests, contacts no servers, loads no remote content, and includes no advertising or analytics libraries. The entire game, including all graphics and sounds, is generated on your device.

Advertising and purchases

There is no advertising in Pulse Streak and there are no in-app purchases or payments of any kind. Nothing in the app costs money, and no payment information is ever requested.

Children's privacy

Pulse Streak is suitable for children, and it is designed so that there is nothing to protect: we collect no personal data from anyone, of any age. There are no accounts, no chat, no user-to-user contact, no user-generated content shared with others, no advertising, and no tracking.

Because no personal data of a child (or of any user) is collected or processed, no parental consent mechanism is required under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or under comparable children's privacy laws elsewhere. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the app listing will state it.

Permissions

The app requests no permission that can access your personal information — no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or phone permissions. The Android application framework used to build the app declares the standard INTERNET permission, but the app makes no use of it: it issues no network requests whatsoever.

Third parties

The game is built on the Phaser 3 game engine, which is bundled inside the app and runs entirely on your device. It is not a service, it collects nothing, and no data is shared with its authors or with anyone else. No other third-party code is included.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete on request. You remain in complete control of the only copy of your game data: clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything, immediately and permanently.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change — for example if advertising, analytics, online leaderboards, or accounts were added — this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date above revised before the new version is released.
