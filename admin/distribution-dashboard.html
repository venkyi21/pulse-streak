Two things to settle before chasing reach

You cannot measure any of this. The app ships with no analytics by design, which is the right call for a children's app on a store — but it means 10,000 players would teach you nothing about where they quit or whether streaks retain anyone. The web build is the one place you could add cookieless, privacy-preserving analytics without touching the store version. That is a decision worth making deliberately rather than by default.

No child has played it yet. Reach amplifies whatever you already have. If the game does not hold a seven-year-old for three runs, distribution spends attention you only get once. Five kids and an afternoon still outranks every row in the table above.
